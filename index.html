Previous Week's Levels (XAU/USD)

High/Low from Sunday's Globex open to Friday's Globex close (17:00 → 16:00 Chicago; in Tehran time Mon 01:30 → Sat 00:30 in US summer, Mon 02:30 → Sat 01:30 in US winter). Spot Close is computed automatically as the close of the last candle in that window — not a live tick. Futures Close is auto-filled from the newest futures contract seen in the 5 days' futures fields (the contract next week trades on); you can still paste or type it. In a roll week the older contract is added as an extra "expiring" row for reference.
